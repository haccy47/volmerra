About Volmerra

Volmerra is the official website of a fictional story universe — one universe, one continuing story, told in chapters.

A single universe, not a shelf of separate books.

Everything published about Volmerra — the story, its characters and its world — lives here, in one place. The site is organised around the story itself rather than around a catalogue: read the published chapters, meet the characters, and explore the universe as it is revealed.

Reading is open to everyone. Creating a free account adds your personal reading library: progress on chapters you are reading, favorites, saved chapters and reading history, kept separately for each reader.

Accounts

Reader accounts

Registration & sessions

Readers create their own account with a display name, email and password. Sessions persist between visits until you sign out. There are no demo or shared accounts — every profile belongs to a real, self-registered reader.

Profiles

Each account has a display name and an optional profile picture, shown next to your reading activity and, later, your comments.

Personal library

Continue reading, reading history, favorite chapters and saved chapters are stored privately, per account. Nothing in your library is visible to other readers.
