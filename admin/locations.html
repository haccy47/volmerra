First-run setup

This preview build has no backend yet, so administrator access is provisioned locally: the first signed-in account to claim ownership becomes the site administrator, and the claim is closed afterwards. In production, administrator roles must be granted and every admin operation must be authorized server-side.

Manage locations

Prototype note: locations are stored through the client-side data layer adapter. The public Places page (pages/places.html) groups them by their exact region value; locations without a region are listed under "Unassigned Locations" here and in a separate group on the public page — they never disappear.
