First-run setup

This preview build has no backend yet, so administrator access is provisioned locally: the first signed-in account to claim ownership becomes the site administrator, and the claim is closed afterwards. In production, administrator roles must be granted and every admin operation must be authorized server-side.

Scheduled chapters

Scheduled publishing is a prototype: while the admin panel or the public website is open, scheduled chapters are published automatically when their time arrives, and the countdowns above update on their own. Reliable publication when nobody has the website open requires a server-side scheduler in the future backend — a countdown alone does not publish a chapter.
